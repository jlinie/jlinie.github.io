**When:** 2023–2024, during my bachelor's at Sabancı University

**Tech:** Python, TensorFlow, RNNs (recurrent neural networks), model quantization

## The Problem

For diabetic patients, knowing where blood sugar is heading in the next hours
can make a real difference in daily decisions. This project used medical time
series data to forecast glucose levels ahead of time.

## What I Built

<TextWithImage image="/images/projects/p3-detail.jpg">
  I created recurrent neural network models to capture the temporal patterns
  in glucose measurements, then went a step further: applying quantization
  and optimization techniques to shrink the model. In a medical context, a
  model that's small, fast, and reliable beats a marginally more accurate
  giant — devices are constrained, and predictions need to be there when the
  patient needs them.
</TextWithImage>

## Why It Matters to Me

This is the project that pulled me toward time series and efficient deep
learning in the first place. It taught me that the hardest and most
interesting ML problems appear exactly where data is sensitive, noisy, and
resources are limited — a theme I'm still chasing in my master's.
