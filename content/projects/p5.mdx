**When:** During my bachelor's at Sabancı University

**Context:** Industry collaboration with Arçelik (one of Europe's largest home appliance manufacturers)

**Tech:** Python, machine learning for regression/classification on materials data

## The Problem

Phase Change Materials (PCMs) store and release thermal energy, making them
key to energy-efficient refrigerator design. But assessing PCM quality
through physical testing is slow and expensive — could machine learning
predict quality from material properties instead?

## What I Built

<TextWithImage image="/images/projects/p5-detail.jpg">
  I employed machine learning to create a predictive model for PCM quality
  assessment, helping engineers narrow down candidate materials before
  committing to physical tests. The result: a faster, data-informed selection
  process for optimal materials in refrigerator design.
</TextWithImage>

## What I Learned

Working with a real industrial partner meant messy real-world data, domain
experts to communicate with, and predictions that actual engineering
decisions would rest on. It was my first taste of ML leaving the notebook
and entering the real world.
