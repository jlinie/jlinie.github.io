**When:** Summer semester 2026 — ongoing (deadline September 2026)

**Context:** Course project for my deep learning class at TU Darmstadt

**Tech:** Python, PyTorch, Pandas, Hugging Face (dataset & leaderboard)

## The Task

Predict the future hourly operational load index for many parallel time
series at once. The forecast horizon is 336 hours per series, filled by
rolling a 24-step model forward — so small errors compound fast, and
architecture choices really matter.

Models are scored against hidden validation labels on a public leaderboard
using MAE, MSE, RMSE, MAPE, sMAPE, and WAPE. Competing against fellow
students in real time is equal parts motivating and humbling.
