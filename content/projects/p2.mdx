**Context:** Practical lab course at TU Darmstadt

**Tech:** Python, JAX

## What It's About

In this practical lab I work with JAX-based reimplementations of classic
Atari environments, built for reinforcement learning research. The core idea:
rewriting game environments in pure JAX makes them massively parallelizable
on GPU, which speeds up RL experimentation by orders of magnitude compared to
traditional emulator-based setups.
